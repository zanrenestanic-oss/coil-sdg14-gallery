01 / UNDERSTAND

The problem beneath the surface.

Marine plastic pollution is a serious threat to ocean ecosystems. Explain how plastic reaches the sea, how it affects wildlife and why prevention matters.

OUR CHALLENGE

Plastic pollution is harming our oceans.

Plastic bags, bottles, fishing gear and tiny plastic fragments can enter rivers and oceans. Marine animals may swallow plastic or become entangled in it. Plastic can remain in the environment for a very long time and damage habitats.

Add your own research, local examples and reliable sources here. Explain how plastic pollution affects marine animals, habitats and people.
